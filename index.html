Who is Park Jimin?

Lead Vocalist, Main Dancer

Jimin or Park Ji-min (born October 13, 1995) is a member of the South Korean boy band, BTS. He is from Busan, South Korea but moved to Seoul to pursue his passion. He was in fact the last member to join the group and is the main dancer and the lead vocalist in BTS. He has performed solo work with BTS such as "Lie", "Serendipity", "Filter" and outside of BTS "Promise", "Christmas Love".

Jimin's debut solo album, Face, released on March 24, broke records by debuting at number one in South Korea and Japan, and number two in the US, making him the highest-charting Korean solo artist ever on the Billboard 200. Its singles, "Set Me Free Pt. 2" and "Like Crazy", debuted on the Hot 100 at numbers 30 and 1, respectively, with Jimin becoming the first South Korean soloist to top the chart. He also became the first Korean solo artist to top the Billboard Artist 100. His second album, Muse, released on July 19 during his military service, included "Smeraldo Garden Marching Band" and "Who," the latter debuting atop both of Billboard's global charts.
